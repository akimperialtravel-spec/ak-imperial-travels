General Policy

Cancellation and refund rules depend on the airline, visa/service provider, insurer, forex provider, hotel, tour operator or other supplier. Applicable non-refundable amounts and cancellation charges will be communicated before confirmation wherever applicable.

Flight Bookings

Flight cancellations, changes and refunds are subject to the fare rules and airline conditions applicable to the ticket. Applicable airline penalties, fare differences and service charges may apply.

Government/embassy fees and applicable service charges may be non-refundable once submitted or processed. Visa approval is not guaranteed by AK Imperial Travels.

Refund Processing Timeline

Where a refund is approved and due, AK Imperial Travels will generally process the eligible refund within 7–15 business days after approval and/or receipt of the applicable refund from the relevant service provider. Actual credit time may vary depending on the payment provider and banking channels.
